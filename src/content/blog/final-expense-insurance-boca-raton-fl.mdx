## What Boca Raton families should know about funeral costs

Funeral and burial costs in Palm Beach County generally track with statewide Florida averages, though South Florida costs, and Boca Raton specifically, can run somewhat higher depending on the funeral home and services chosen. Final expense insurance exists to make sure that cost doesn't land on your family unexpectedly.

## Coverage amounts Boca Raton residents commonly consider

| Coverage amount | Often considered for |
|---|---|
| $15,000 | Standard funeral costs with genuine cushion |
| $20,000 | Funeral costs plus outstanding bills |
| $25,000 | Maximum cushion for funeral costs and other expenses |

Given the somewhat higher cost of living in Boca Raton, many residents we hear from lean toward the upper end of the typical coverage range, though the right amount still comes down to your specific plans and budget.

## Getting covered without a trip down Federal Highway

Boca Raton's spread-out layout means an in-person insurance meeting can mean real travel time depending on where you live. The entire process — quote, compare, apply — can happen online instead, on your own schedule.

## What Boca Raton applicants should know about eligibility

- **No medical exam** required for most final expense products
- **Simplified issue** available for applicants in reasonably good health, based on a short health questionnaire
- **Guaranteed issue** available regardless of health history, with no health questions asked
- Coverage is available to **Florida residents statewide**, including Palm Beach and Broward counties

## Why final expense coverage matters alongside broader estate planning

Boca Raton is home to plenty of residents with more extensive estate plans already in place. Even so, many of these same families still choose dedicated final expense coverage specifically because it pays out quickly and directly to a named beneficiary, without waiting on probate or broader estate settlement. It's less about overall financial planning and more about making sure someone has immediate, unrestricted funds the moment they're needed.

## Health eligibility for Boca Raton applicants

Whatever your health history looks like, it's worth checking your actual eligibility rather than assuming a condition rules you out. Common, well-managed conditions frequently still qualify for simplified issue pricing, and guaranteed issue remains available regardless of health history for anyone whose situation is more complex.

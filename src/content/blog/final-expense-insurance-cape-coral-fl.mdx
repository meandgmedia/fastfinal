## What Cape Coral applicants should know about eligibility

- **No medical exam** required for most final expense products
- **Simplified issue** available for applicants in reasonably good health, based on a short health questionnaire
- **Guaranteed issue** available regardless of health history, with no health questions asked
- Coverage is available to **Florida residents statewide**, including Lee and Charlotte counties

## A note for Cape Coral's growing retiree population

Cape Coral has drawn a steady wave of retirees in recent years, many without an established relationship with a local funeral home or insurance agent. Final expense insurance doesn't require one — it's a standalone policy that pays a cash benefit directly to your named beneficiary, who can work with any funeral home or provider they choose when the time comes.

## Health eligibility for Cape Coral applicants

Whatever your health history looks like, it's worth checking your actual eligibility rather than assuming a condition rules you out. Common, well-managed conditions frequently still qualify for simplified issue pricing, and guaranteed issue remains available regardless of health history for anyone whose situation is more complex.

## What Clearwater families should know about funeral costs

Funeral and burial costs in Pinellas County generally track with statewide Florida averages, though the specific number depends heavily on the funeral home, the type of service, and whether you're planning burial or cremation. Final expense insurance exists to make sure that cost doesn't land on your family unexpectedly, whatever it ends up being. For a community with as many transplants and seasonal residents as Clearwater has, having a plan already in place — rather than figuring it out after the fact — tends to make a difficult moment noticeably simpler for whoever's left to handle things.

## What Clearwater applicants should know about eligibility

- **No medical exam** required for most final expense products
- **Simplified issue** available for applicants in reasonably good health, based on a short health questionnaire
- **Guaranteed issue** available regardless of health history, with no health questions asked
- Coverage is available to **Florida residents statewide**, including Pinellas and Hillsborough counties

## What actually determines your price in Coral Springs

Location isn't a pricing factor for final expense insurance — the same core factors apply whether you live in Coral Springs or anywhere else in Florida. Your age is the single biggest driver, followed by tobacco use, your health answers if you're applying for simplified issue, and the coverage amount you choose. Comparing real quotes matters more than researching general local pricing.

## What Coral Springs applicants should know about eligibility

- **No medical exam** required for most final expense products
- **Simplified issue** available for applicants in reasonably good health, based on a short health questionnaire
- **Guaranteed issue** available regardless of health history, with no health questions asked at all
- Coverage is available to **Florida residents statewide**, including Broward and Palm Beach counties

## Health eligibility for Coral Springs applicants

Whatever your health history looks like, it's worth checking your actual eligibility rather than assuming a condition rules you out. Common, well-managed conditions frequently still qualify for simplified issue pricing, and guaranteed issue remains available regardless of health history for anyone whose situation is more complex.

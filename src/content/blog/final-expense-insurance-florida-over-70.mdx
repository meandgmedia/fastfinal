If you're over 70 and just now looking into final expense insurance, you might be bracing yourself for bad news — assuming coverage is either unavailable or unaffordable at this point. Neither is necessarily true. Final expense insurance is one of the few types of coverage genuinely built with applicants in your age range in mind.

## Is 70 too old to apply?

No. Many final expense products remain available well into the 80s, and 70 is comfortably within the range most carriers design for. This isn't a niche exception — it's a core part of who these products are built for in the first place.

## What does change at this age

Age is one of the biggest factors in your premium, so someone applying at 70 will generally see a higher monthly cost than someone applying at 45 for the same coverage amount. That's standard across the industry, not something specific to Florida or to any one carrier. The practical takeaway: if you're considering this at all, applying sooner rather than later tends to lock in a better rate, since waiting doesn't make the price go down.

## What coverage typically looks like for applicants over 70

Most people in this age range we hear from choose coverage somewhere between $10,000 and $15,000, aimed primarily at covering funeral and burial costs. There's no universal right number — it comes down to what you want covered and what fits comfortably into your budget.

## Health questions at this age

For simplified issue products, health questions become more relevant the longer your health history, but plenty of common, well-managed conditions don't disqualify you. If your health history is more complicated, guaranteed issue remains available — no health questions asked, with eligibility based on age and Florida residency rather than your medical history.

| Situation | What's often still available |
|---|---|
| Generally healthy, few conditions | Simplified issue, often better pricing |
| Some managed chronic conditions | Simplified issue is still frequently an option |
| More complex health history | Guaranteed issue remains available |

## Common concerns at this age (and the real answer)

**"I was declined for life insurance years ago — is there any point trying again?"**
Being declined for a large, traditional life insurance policy doesn't mean you'd be declined for final expense insurance, especially guaranteed issue, which doesn't ask health questions at all.

**"Will this affect my Medicare or Social Security?"**
No — final expense insurance is a separate, private product and doesn't interact with either.

**"Is the application process going to be complicated at my age?"**
Not any more than it would be for a younger applicant. The process is built to be simple regardless of age — a handful of questions, a coverage amount, done.

## Frequently asked questions

**What's the oldest age someone can typically apply?**
This varies by product, but many final expense options remain available into the 80s — it's worth checking your specific eligibility rather than assuming a cutoff.

**Do I need family involved in the application?**
Not required, though many people choose to loop in family since the coverage benefits them too.

**Will my premium go up over time?**
Most final expense policies are designed with level premiums for life — the rate you lock in at application generally doesn't increase later.

**What if my health has changed a lot in the last few years?**
That's exactly the kind of situation guaranteed issue is built for — no health questions means recent changes in your health history don't affect eligibility.

## What Fort Lauderdale families should know about funeral costs

Funeral and burial costs in Broward County generally track with statewide Florida averages, though South Florida costs can run somewhat higher depending on the specific funeral home and services chosen. Final expense insurance exists to make sure that cost — whatever it turns out to be — doesn't land on your family unexpectedly.

| Coverage amount | Often considered for |

| $10,000 | A complete, standard funeral service |

Most Fort Lauderdale residents we hear from land in the $15,000 to $25,000 range, reflecting the somewhat higher cost of living in Broward County generally, though the right amount still comes down to your specific plans and budget.

## Getting covered without a trip through Fort Lauderdale traffic

Fort Lauderdale traffic during peak season is legendary, and driving in for an in-person insurance meeting isn't most people's idea of a good use of an afternoon. The entire process — quote, compare, apply — can happen online instead, on your own schedule, without adding a commute to your day.

## What Fort Lauderdale applicants should know about eligibility

- **No medical exam** required for most final expense products
- **Simplified issue** available for applicants in reasonably good health, based on a short health questionnaire
- **Guaranteed issue** available regardless of health history, with no health questions asked at all
- Coverage is available to **Florida residents statewide**, including Broward, Palm Beach, and Miami-Dade counties

## A note on Fort Lauderdale's seasonal population

Like much of South Florida, Fort Lauderdale has a significant seasonal population — residents who split time between Florida and another state, particularly during winter months. If that describes you, final expense insurance eligibility is generally based on Florida residency, so it's worth confirming your specific situation as part of the quote process.

Whatever your health history looks like, it's worth checking your actual eligibility rather than assuming a condition rules you out. Common, well-managed conditions frequently still qualify for simplified issue pricing, and guaranteed issue remains available regardless of health history for anyone whose situation is more complex. The only reliable way to know for certain is going through the quote process itself.

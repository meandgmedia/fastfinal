Gainesville's identity as a university town shapes a lot of the city, but final expense insurance conversations here look a lot like they do anywhere else in Florida — driven mostly by longtime residents, retirees, and families thinking ahead. Here's the local picture.

## What Gainesville families should know about funeral costs

Funeral and burial costs in Alachua County generally track with statewide Florida averages, though the specific number depends on the funeral home, the type of service, and whether you're planning burial or cremation. Final expense insurance exists to make sure that cost doesn't land on your family unexpectedly.

## Coverage amounts Gainesville residents commonly consider

| Coverage amount | Often considered for |
|---|---|
| $5,000 | A basic cremation or simple service |
| $10,000 | A more complete funeral service |
| $15,000 | Funeral costs with some cushion for other expenses |
| $20,000 | Funeral costs plus outstanding bills |

Most Gainesville residents we hear from land somewhere between $10,000 and $20,000, though the right amount depends entirely on what you're trying to cover.

## What Gainesville applicants should know about eligibility

- **No medical exam** required for most final expense products
- **Simplified issue** available for applicants in reasonably good health, based on a short health questionnaire
- **Guaranteed issue** available regardless of health history, with no health questions asked
- Coverage is available to **Florida residents statewide**, including Alachua and Marion counties

## Health eligibility for Gainesville applicants

Whatever your health history looks like, it's worth checking your actual eligibility rather than assuming a condition rules you out. Common, well-managed conditions frequently still qualify for simplified issue pricing, and guaranteed issue remains available regardless of health history for anyone whose situation is more complex.

## What Melbourne families should know about funeral costs

Funeral and burial costs in Brevard County generally track with statewide Florida averages, though the specific number depends on the funeral home, the type of service, and whether you're planning burial or cremation. Final expense insurance exists to make sure that cost doesn't land on your family unexpectedly.

## Getting covered without a drive across Brevard County

Brevard County covers real ground — from Melbourne down to Palm Bay and up toward the Space Coast communities — and driving for an in-person insurance meeting isn't always convenient. The entire process can happen online instead: get a quote, compare your options, apply, all without adding a trip to your day.

## What Melbourne applicants should know about eligibility

- **No medical exam** required for most final expense products
- **Simplified issue** available for applicants in reasonably good health, based on a short health questionnaire
- **Guaranteed issue** available regardless of health history, with no health questions asked
- Coverage is available to **Florida residents statewide**, including Brevard and Indian River counties

## A note for Melbourne's aerospace and military community

With Patrick Space Force Base and a significant aerospace industry presence in the area, many Melbourne residents are veterans, military retirees, or connected to the space industry. If you're a veteran, it's worth knowing that final expense insurance is separate from any VA burial benefits you may be entitled to — the two can work alongside each other rather than one replacing the other, giving your family more complete coverage regardless of what VA benefits ultimately provide.

## Health eligibility for Melbourne applicants

Whatever your health history looks like, it's worth checking your actual eligibility rather than assuming a condition rules you out. Common, well-managed conditions frequently still qualify for simplified issue pricing, and guaranteed issue remains available regardless of health history for anyone whose situation is more complex. The only reliable way to know for certain is going through the quote process itself.

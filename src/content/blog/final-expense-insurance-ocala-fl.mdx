## What Ocala families should know about funeral costs

Funeral and burial costs in Marion County generally track with statewide Florida averages, though the specific number depends on the funeral home, the type of service, and whether you're planning burial or cremation. Final expense insurance exists to make sure that cost doesn't land on your family unexpectedly.

## A note for Ocala's growing retiree population

Ocala has drawn a significant wave of retirees in recent years, many relocating from other states without an established relationship with a local funeral home or insurance agent. Final expense insurance doesn't require one — it's a standalone policy that pays a cash benefit directly to your named beneficiary, who can work with any funeral home or provider they choose.

## What Ocala applicants should know about eligibility

- **No medical exam** required for most final expense products
- **Simplified issue** available for applicants in reasonably good health, based on a short health questionnaire
- **Guaranteed issue** available regardless of health history, with no health questions asked
- Coverage is available to **Florida residents statewide**, including Marion and Alachua counties

## Health eligibility for Ocala applicants

Whatever your health history looks like, it's worth checking your actual eligibility rather than assuming a condition rules you out. Common, well-managed conditions frequently still qualify for simplified issue pricing, and guaranteed issue remains available regardless of health history for anyone whose situation is more complex.

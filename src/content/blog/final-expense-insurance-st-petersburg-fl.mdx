## What St. Petersburg families should know about funeral costs

Funeral and burial costs in Pinellas County generally track with statewide Florida averages, though the specific number depends on the funeral home, the type of service, and whether you're planning burial or cremation. Final expense insurance exists specifically to make sure that cost — whatever it ends up being — doesn't land on your family during an already difficult stretch of time.

## Coverage amounts St. Petersburg residents commonly consider

| Coverage amount | Often considered for |
|---|---|
| $5,000 | A basic cremation or simple service |
| $10,000 | A more complete funeral service |
| $15,000 | Funeral costs with some cushion for other expenses |
| $20,000 | Funeral costs plus outstanding bills |

Most St. Petersburg residents we hear from land somewhere between $10,000 and $20,000, though the right amount depends entirely on what you're trying to cover — a straightforward service looks very different from a larger, more traditional one.

St. Petersburg has grown substantially, and depending on whether you're downtown, near the beaches, or further out, an in-person insurance meeting can mean real travel time. ## What St. Petersburg applicants should know about eligibility

- **No medical exam** required for most final expense products
- **Simplified issue** available for applicants in reasonably good health, based on a short health questionnaire
- **Guaranteed issue** available regardless of health history, with no health questions asked at all
- Coverage is available to **Florida residents statewide**, including Pinellas and Hillsborough counties

## A note for St. Petersburg's growing retiree population

St. Petersburg has drawn a steady wave of new retirees in recent years, many without an established relationship with a local funeral home or insurance agent the way longtime residents might have. Final expense insurance doesn't require one — it's a standalone policy that pays a cash benefit directly to your named beneficiary, who can work with any funeral home or provider they choose when the time comes. That flexibility matters in a city where so many residents are relatively new arrivals.

## Health eligibility for St. Petersburg applicants

Whatever your health history looks like, it's worth checking your actual eligibility rather than assuming a condition rules you out. Common, well-managed conditions frequently still qualify for simplified issue pricing, and guaranteed issue remains available regardless of health history for anyone whose situation is more complex. The only reliable way to know for certain is going through the quote process itself, rather than guessing based on a diagnosis alone.

## St. Petersburg's downtown growth and what it means for planning

St. Petersburg's downtown and waterfront districts have seen significant development over the past decade, drawing both younger professionals and retirees looking for a walkable, amenity-rich lifestyle. That growth has meant rising costs across the board locally, funeral and burial services included. Having final expense coverage in place ahead of time — rather than assuming costs will stay static — is one practical way to keep pace with a city that's changed noticeably even in the last several years.

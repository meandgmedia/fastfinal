## What actually determines your price in Vero Beach

Location isn't a pricing factor for final expense insurance — the same core factors apply whether you live in Vero Beach or anywhere else in Florida. Your age is the single biggest driver, followed by tobacco use, your health answers if you're applying for simplified issue, and the coverage amount you choose.

## A note for Vero Beach's established retiree community

Vero Beach has one of the more established retiree populations on the Treasure Coast, with many residents having lived in the area for decades. Final expense insurance doesn't require a lengthy relationship with a specific local provider — it's a standalone policy that works with any funeral home your family chooses.

## What Vero Beach applicants should know about eligibility

- **No medical exam** required for most final expense products
- **Simplified issue** available for applicants in reasonably good health, based on a short health questionnaire
- **Guaranteed issue** available regardless of health history, with no health questions asked at all
- Coverage is available to **Florida residents statewide**, including Indian River and St. Lucie counties

## Health eligibility for Vero Beach applicants

Whatever your health history looks like, it's worth checking your actual eligibility rather than assuming a condition rules you out. Common, well-managed conditions frequently still qualify for simplified issue pricing, and guaranteed issue remains available regardless of health history for anyone whose situation is more complex.

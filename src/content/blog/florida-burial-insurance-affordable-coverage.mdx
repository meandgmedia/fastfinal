Funerals in Florida aren't cheap. Between the service, the casket or urn, the cemetery plot, and everything in between, most families are looking at somewhere in the ballpark of $7,000 to $12,000 — sometimes more, depending on the county and the choices made. Burial insurance exists to make sure that bill doesn't land on your family's shoulders.

Here's the thing nobody tells you upfront: burial insurance is affordable. Not "affordable" in the way a car salesman says it — actually affordable, because the coverage amount is small and targeted.

## What burial insurance covers

Burial insurance pays a cash death benefit to whoever you name as your beneficiary. That's the important part — it's not paid to a funeral home directly, it's paid to *your person*, who can then use it however makes sense:

- Funeral home services and staff fees
- Casket or urn
- Cemetery plot and headstone
- Transportation
- Obituary and other arrangements
- Literally anything else — the money isn't restricted

That last point matters more than people realize. If there's money left over after the funeral, your family keeps it. No strings attached.

## Why "affordable" is actually true here

Burial insurance is priced for the coverage amount you're buying — usually somewhere between $5,000 and $25,000 — not the six-figure amounts traditional life insurance deals in. Smaller coverage amount, smaller premium. It's genuinely one of the more budget-friendly types of insurance you can buy, especially if you apply while you're younger.

## How much coverage do you actually need?

There's no universal right answer, but here's a reasonable way to think about it:

| Coverage amount | Might make sense if... |
|---|---|
| $5,000 | You want a simple cremation covered |
| $10,000 | You want a fuller service covered |
| $15,000–$20,000 | You want the funeral plus some breathing room for other bills |
| $25,000 | You want broader coverage for final expenses overall |

The smartest move is comparing a couple of amounts side-by-side rather than guessing. It only takes a couple minutes.

## Getting approved isn't as hard as people assume

A lot of Florida residents put this off because they assume their health will get them denied. Two things worth knowing:

1. **Simplified issue** products ask a short list of health questions but skip the exam — and plenty of manageable conditions don't disqualify you.
2. **Guaranteed issue** products skip health questions entirely. Slightly different pricing structure, but approval isn't based on your health history at all.

If you got turned down for traditional life insurance years ago, that doesn't mean burial insurance is off the table.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is burial insurance different from final expense insurance?**
Not really — they're two names for the same type of policy. Read the full comparison [here](/blog/final-expense-vs-burial-insurance-florida/).

**Do I need a medical exam?**
Typically no, for either simplified or guaranteed issue burial insurance.

**Can my family use the money for something other than the funeral?**
Yes. The payout goes to your beneficiary, and they decide how to use it.

**How do I get a quote?**

## Bottom line

Burial insurance is one of the simpler financial decisions you'll make this year — small coverage amount, manageable price, and it takes a real burden off your family. The only hard part is deciding to get the quote.

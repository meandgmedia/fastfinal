Everyone wants a straight number when they ask this question, and the honest answer is: it depends on a handful of specific, knowable factors — not a mystery, just individual to you. Let's actually break it down instead of dodging the question.

## The five things that actually move your price

### 1. Your age

The single biggest factor. Younger applicants pay less, generally, and premiums are typically level for life — meaning the rate you lock in doesn't creep up as you age.

### 2. Tobacco use

Tobacco users typically pay more than non-users for identical coverage. This is standard across the entire life insurance industry, not a Florida quirk.

### 3. Health (for simplified issue products)

Your answers to a short health questionnaire affect your rate class. Guaranteed issue policies skip this entirely, which is part of why they cost more per dollar of coverage.

### 4. Coverage amount

More coverage, higher premium. Obvious, but worth stating plainly — the real question isn't "what's cheapest" but "what actually covers what I need."

### 5. Policy type

Simplified issue vs. guaranteed issue changes the math meaningfully. Simplified issue tends to be more affordable if you qualify; guaranteed issue trades a higher per-dollar cost for skipping health questions.

## Why two Florida neighbors can get wildly different quotes

This trips people up constantly. Your neighbor's $10,000 policy costing $28/month and yours costing $41/month for the same amount doesn't mean something's wrong — it usually just means you're different ages, different tobacco status, or qualified for a different rate class. Comparing notes with friends is a nice way to start a conversation, but a lousy way to estimate your own price.

## How to actually keep costs down

- **Apply sooner rather than later** — age is the biggest lever, and waiting doesn't help
- **Answer health questions accurately** — it gets you correctly priced from the start, avoiding issues later
- **Compare a couple of coverage amounts** — sometimes dropping from $20,000 to $15,000 saves meaningfully without leaving you underinsured
- **Compare simplified vs. guaranteed issue** — if you qualify for simplified issue, it's often the more affordable route
